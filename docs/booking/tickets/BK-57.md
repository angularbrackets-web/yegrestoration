## 8a · 🔴 THE FIRST LIVE PROBE READ ZERO FOR BOTH, AND THAT WAS THE PROBE'S FAULT

**The first `curl` reported the old claim absent AND the new sentence absent** —
a result that should be impossible on a page that renders the answer at all.
**It was `curl` without `-L`:** the site **301s to `www.`**, so the probe was
grepping a redirect stub, **not the page.**

🔴 ***A probe that fetches the wrong document reports "claim removed" and
"claim never arrived" identically, and the first of those is the answer you were
hoping for.*** *Both counts were zero, and a check that only asserted the OLD
claim was gone would have PASSED on a redirect stub.*

**→ This is `CLAUDE.md`'s BK-48 rule in a new place** — *"write the probe so it
produces the artifact rather than something adjacent to it."* **The two-column
form above is what makes it safe: the positive column cannot be satisfied by a
stub.** *(Same shape as `V5` in `§5`: a negative check with no positive twin
passes on an empty document. Third instance in this session.)*
