# BK-15 — Booking widget overflow: fieldsets escape the card

**Status:** committed 2026-08-12 (same-day: found in the user's BK-14
post-deploy pass, diagnosed, fixed, gates green).
**Phase:** P6 · **Tier:** Light (styling — gates only) · **Depends on:**
nothing · **Blocks:** nothing (BK-18, the picker redesign, supersedes this
area later but must not regress it).

Client-reported 2026-08-12 with screenshots: on `/book/`, the day chips and
the time-slot grid render past the white card's right edge, on desktop and
mobile.

## Diagnosis — measured, not guessed

The step content sits in `<fieldset>` elements, and fieldsets carry a browser
default of `min-inline-size: min-content`: a fieldset refuses to shrink below
its content's natural width, and `width: auto` on a block box yields to
`min-width`. The day strip is a flex row of ~15 `shrink-0` chips, so its
min-content width is far wider than the card — the fieldset ballooned to it,
which simultaneously (a) pushed the strip and the time grid past the card
edge and (b) defeated the strip's own `overflow-x-auto` (a scroll container
only scrolls when something *outside* it constrains its width; the fieldset
never did). Measured live before the fix at 1440px viewport: card 720px wide
ending at x=1080; fieldset, strip, and grid all 900px ending at x=1301 —
221px of visible spill — with computed `min-inline-size: min-content` on the
fieldset and the strip's `scrollWidth (1179) > clientWidth (900)` scrolling
nothing.

## Fix

`min-w-0` added to all four step fieldsets in
`src/components/BookingForm.svelte` (`border-0 p-0 m-0` → `border-0 p-0 m-0
min-w-0`). Only step 1 overflows today, but the other three carry the same
latent default and one of them is a flex/grid parent away from the same bug.

Measured after, same viewport: fieldset 638px inside the 720px card
(respecting its padding), the day strip genuinely scrolls
(`scrollWidth > clientWidth` with clipping at the card edge), and at 390px
mobile width `document.body.scrollWidth === window.innerWidth` (no page-level
horizontal scroll); screenshot eyeballed at both widths.

## Out of scope

- Any visual redesign of the picker — that is BK-18 (Calendly-style layout),
  which owns aesthetics including the strip's visible scrollbar.
- The admin entry form's fieldsets, if any — different surface, and no
  overflow reported there.
