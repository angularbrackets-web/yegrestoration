## The decision this ticket exists to make safe

**Client decision, 2026-09-01:** when a request is never reviewed and its slot
passes, **the slot is simply lost.** No automatic cancellation, no email, the
business absorbs it. This is option (ii) of the question
`PREPAY-PLAN-2026-09-01.md` left open.

**The client attached three conditions**, and they are the whole of this ticket:

1. a **Status column** on the Upcoming table,
2. an **unreviewed count**,
3. **`RECEIVED_TIMING_LINE`** on the booking form's fallback card.

The plan's minimum list had five items. **Two are deliberately excluded** — see
"What is out of scope and why".

## Why Reviewed rather than Light

Nothing here is a write path, and two of three changes are an admin display.
Light would be the mechanical reading. It is refused because **this ticket is the
condition on an irreversible change**: if it ships incomplete and the sweep is
deleted anyway, a real customer holds a slot nobody will look at, with no email
and no in-product signal to the office. The tier follows the consequence, not
the diff size.

Plan review vindicated this. **Six blockers on a ~40-line display change**, and
five of them were assertions that would have passed without the feature working.

## Risk areas — reviewers, weight your attention here

1. **Assertions that are green before the code is written.** The single largest
   category in plan review. `index.astro` already contains every needle a naive
   A1–A4 would use, in the **Past** table. Any assertion here must be slice-based
   and must prove its slice was found.
2. **Copy-inventory trap.** Item 3 adds a claim to a surface. Grep the claim,
   not the constant; check sibling surfaces for order and contradiction.
3. **Negative-assertion trap (BK-48).** Ask what each pin literally matches.
4. **Red-first honesty (BK-49/W0).** Score on **(exit code, summary line)**.

5. **Statements that go false when sweep 2 dies.** C2 ships a new sentence. It
   must not contain a mechanism claim.

## What is wrong

### 1. The office cannot see the state that now matters

`src/pages/admin/appointments/index.astro:169-177` — the Upcoming headers are
**Slot · Name · Phone · Service · City · Route · Files** plus a blank action
column. **There is no Status column.**

`statusClasses` is defined at `:69` and called exactly once, at `:293` — inside
the **Past** table. `STATUS_LABELS` is imported at `:11` and used only by
`pastStatusLabel` at `:93`.

On the only screen the office triages from, a `pending_review` row and a
`confirmed` row are **visually identical**. That was survivable while cron sweep
2 swept unanswered requests out at `slot − 4h`. Under the client's decision
nothing sweeps, and the distinction becomes the most important fact on the row.

The colour vocabulary already exists and already means the right thing —
`statusClasses`' comment (`:59-68`, sentence at `:62-65`):

> *"AMBER is 'this is waiting on you'. Both new live states get it, because both
> are somebody's inaction with a deadline attached — an unreviewed request and
> an unpaid approval."*

The screen that most needs that sentence is the one that does not render it.

### 2. Nothing counts the unreviewed

`:53` computes `warningCount`; `:140-145` renders it in red under the page
title. It is the existing shape for "the office must look at N things", and
there is exactly one of them. An unreviewed request is now a second such thing
with no counter.

### 3. The customer's only safety net is missing from one of three surfaces

`RECEIVED_TIMING_LINE` (`src/lib/booking-copy.ts:657`):

> *"If you have not heard from us and the appointment is close, call or text us
> — do not wait."*

Its doc comment says why it exists — the client refused to publish a review SLA,
so this line is what the customer gets instead:

> *"What this line does instead is give the customer an action for the case the
> intention fails, which is the only part of an SLA that helps them."*

Under the old behaviour "the intention fails" was bounded — the sweep declined
the request and sent an email. **Under the client's decision it can fail silently
and permanently.** The line stops being a courtesy and becomes the customer's
entire recourse.
