# BK-45 — The email a paying customer keeps carries none of the terms they accepted

**Status:** draft 2026-08-19
**Phase:** P9 (post-deploy) · **Tier:** Reviewed — copy and customer-facing
record of a completed purchase · **Deploy:** 3. **Migration:** none.

## What the customer actually receives after paying

Found 2026-08-19 by the first real end-to-end payment on production (#35).

`markPaid()` sends **`planFirstConfirmationEmail`** (`booking-payment.ts:674`),
which is `planBoundaryEmail` — a calendar-boundary message. Its entire body is:

```
heading + lead + a 4-row table (When/Where/Service/Reference)
        + calendar line + phone line
```

**No assessment terms. No have-ready list. No visit length. No "You chose".**

## Why that contradicts what the code says about itself

`booking-email.ts` states the intent directly, in the block BK-36 edited:

> *"the confirmed message carries it because it is the one the customer keeps
> after paying, and terms that vanish from the record of a completed purchase
> are terms nobody can point at afterwards."*

That reasoning is about `customerConfirmation({ messageType: 'confirmed' })` —
which carries the terms, the have-ready list and the chosen tier. **But nothing
on the payment path sends it.** `planForAppointment` defaults `messageType` to
`'confirmed'` (`booking-admin-notify.ts:109`), so that message is what the
**"Resend confirmation"** button sends, and only that.

So there are **two materially different emails both called the confirmation**:

| | Automatic, on payment | Manual, "Resend confirmation" |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Builder | `planFirstConfirmationEmail` | `customerConfirmation` |
| Heading | "Your assessment is confirmed" | "You're booked" |
| Fee terms | ❌ | ✅ |
| Have-ready list | ❌ | ✅ |
| "You chose: …" | ❌ | ✅ |
| ICS | ✅ | ✅ |

The customer who pays gets the first. The customer whose office clicks Resend
gets a different, fuller message under a different heading.

**And `booking-email.ts:629-630` is stale in a way that hid this:** it says the
invite *"goes out behind the payment, from `messageType: 'confirmed'`"*. It does
not — it goes out from `planFirstConfirmationEmail`. A comment naming a path
that is not taken is how the gap stayed invisible.

## Severity — moderate, and stated honestly

It is **not** a blocker and it is **not** an information blackout:

- the customer received the full terms in the **request** email, days earlier;
- `/book/confirmed/` renders the have-ready list immediately after payment;
- the confirmation carries the operationally critical facts — time, address,
  reference, and a working ICS.

What is genuinely lost is that **the email a customer re-opens the night before
the visit** is the thinnest of the three. The have-ready list is the most
practically useful content in the whole flow and it is absent from the one
message they will actually go back to. And the record of what they paid for — the
tier, the amount, the terms — lives only in an email sent before they paid.

## The question this must answer first

**Should the two messages be merged, or should the automatic one simply grow?**
Not obvious, and worth one decision rather than a patch:

- Merging means one confirmation, one heading, one place to edit — and kills the
  "Resend sends a different email than the original" surprise the office has
  today.
- Growing `planBoundaryEmail` risks dragging fee terms into the **cancellation**
  and **restore** messages, which share that builder and must not carry them.

The second risk is the real constraint: whatever ships must not put assessment
terms into a cancellation email.

## Scope

- Decide the merge-or-grow question above and record it in the Assumptions log.
- The post-payment email carries at minimum the **have-ready list** and the
  **chosen tier and amount**; the terms are the decision above.
- Fix `booking-email.ts:629-630`'s stale comment.
- Make "Resend confirmation" send the same message the customer originally got,
  or say plainly in the UI that it does not.

## Risk areas for the reviewer

- `planBoundaryEmail` is shared by cancel / restore / first-confirmation. Any
  growth must be per-caller, not in the shared builder.
- BK-36's email assertions currently cover `customerConfirmation` for both
  message types. If the payment path changes builder, those pins must follow it
  or they will keep proving a message nobody receives.
- The ICS must not change — the calendar side of this works today.
