# BK-54 — Snapshot the terms at insert, and render the resend from the snapshot

**Status:** 🆕 **OPENED 2026-09-05, not planned, not started.** Named by BK-53
revision 4 (R4-3) as *"the real fix"* for R2-B2, and by decision 16 as the one
structure that survives an arbitrary number of pricing flips.
**This ticket exists so R4-3's option (a) has a home. It is NOT a gate on BK-53
or BK-55**, both of which ship option (b) — suppression — as the interim.

**Phase:** free-booking changeover · **Tier:** ⚠️ **Reviewed + FULL ADVERSARIAL
PASS.** CLAUDE.md's trigger is met outright: this **touches the appointment
insert**.
**Depends on:** BK-55 shipping option (b) first. **Blocks:** nothing.
**Target branch:** decide at planning — it is *not* part of the BK-51 push.

## The problem, in one paragraph

`appointments` records **that** the customer ticked the terms box
(`terms_acked_at`) but not **what the box said**. Post-flip acks attest to
different text under an identical schema, and only `created_at` and git history
separate them. So any message rebuilt later — the Resend button is the live
instance — renders **today's** constants against **yesterday's** consent.
BK-55 §3 suppresses the block for pre-cutoff rows and points the customer at
their original email. That is honest; it is not a record.

## Scope, as inherited

- A `terms_version` (or a snapshot column) written **at insert**, alongside
  `terms_acked_at`.
- `resend.ts` and any other rebuild path render **from the snapshot**, not from
  `booking-copy.ts`.
- The thirteen migration-008 pre-prepay rows have no snapshot and never will.
  🔴 **CORRECTED 2026-09-06 — this line said they "must route to BK-55's pointer
  sentence" and that is now FALSE.** BK-55 §H-1 established that those rows are
  **web** bookings (`008-review-lifecycle.ts:162`: *"13 historical appointments"*
  confirmed *"under a flow that took no money online"*), so telling them *"the
  terms you accepted are in your original confirmation email"* is false twice —
  they accepted nothing, and their original email had no terms block. **They route
  to `'none-legacy'`, which says only *"Our current terms are on the website."***
  *(This ticket encoded the exact falsehood the sibling amendment was written to
  remove, which is what a handoff to a ticket nobody re-reads looks like.)*
  ⚠️ **R1-D2's three-state problem rides here too:** `null` / `0` / `>0` are three
  different answers and a `?.` chain collapses two of them.

## Why this is the structural fix — the strongest argument, recorded here rather than in a sibling

**BK-55 §H-4.** Its suppression handles *which era of terms* a row agreed to. It
does **not** handle the **post-flip hand-charged** row: that row is correctly
classified `'current'`, so it receives today's free-mode terms — *"the visit is
free… a travel fee may apply"* — **beside a real charge, with no refund policy at
all**, because decision 19 moved the charged-path terms into `APPROVED_*` and
`resend.ts` never sends `APPROVED_*`.

**That is BK-55 §C's opening sentence, verbatim, for a population §C ticks as
correctly classified.** And decision 19's own argument says the population
**grows**: the travel fee makes hand-charging expected rather than rare.

🔴 **A snapshot at insert is the only fix that closes it**, because the question
stops being *"when did they agree"* and becomes *"what did they agree to"* — which
is the one form that survives an arbitrary number of pricing flips (decision 16).
**BK-55 §H-4's G11 is the interim: a `'current'` row with `settled.totalCents > 0`
must render the charged-path terms.**
- Retire BK-55 §3's suppression only when the snapshot covers every row that can
  reach the Resend button — **not on the day the column lands.**

## Why it is not smuggled into a copy ticket

R2's lane C called it *"the single highest-value item"* in the whole changeover.
It is an **insert-path** change with a migration, and BK-53/BK-55 are a copy
deploy on a branch that already carries BK-51. Combining them would put a schema
change inside the push that flips the pricing model.
