## §1 · 🔴 The request email prices the assessment — and the proposed fix was wrong

### The defect, verified

`booking-email.ts:411` forces `settled = null` on a request, so `tierLine`
(**`:460-464`**, *not* `:456-462` as inherited) takes the `assessmentSummary`
branch for **every request email — the first message every web customer
receives**:

```ts
460  const tierLine = input.assessmentTier
461    ? `You chose: ${
462        settled !== null ? tierNameOf(input.assessmentTier) : assessmentSummary(input)
463      }`
464    : null;
```

`assessmentSummary` (**`:1159-1169`**) recomputes from the pricing table:

```ts
1166  const name = ASSESSMENT_TIER_NAMES[input.assessmentTier];
1167  const weekend = quote.afterHours ? ' — weekend rate, 1.5x' : '';
1168  return `${name} — ${formatCents(quote.baseCents)} + GST (${formatCents(quote.totalCents)} total)${weekend}`;
```

It renders *"You chose: Assessment + written report — $699.00 + GST ($733.95
total) — weekend rate, 1.5x"* **in the same email as "The on-site assessment
visit is free."** ⚠️ **It is computed from `assessmentQuote`, not from any
constant**, so **no copy deletion in BK-53 touches it** and no claim-shape pin
sees it — email bodies never reach `dist/`. **It defeats decision 17 and
decision 21 at once, on the highest-volume surface in either half.**

### 🔴 R4-1's fix — *"`return name;`"* — IS THE WRONG EDIT

**Verified this session, and it is the reason this ticket needed a plan rather
than a copy-paste of R4-1:**

- **`tierNameOf` already exists** at **`:1155-1157`** and already returns exactly
  the bare name. Making `assessmentSummary` `return name;` produces a **duplicate
  of a function three lines above it**.
- 🔴 **`assessmentSummary` has a SECOND caller: `:1214`, in
  `internalNotification` — the OFFICE's message**, guarded by the comment at
  `:1192` recording that BK-46 deliberately makes it the settled figure where one
  exists. **The office is the one party that needs the number**: it is what they
  quote on the confirmation call and what decision 8 has them decide the travel
  fee against. `return name;` strips it from them silently, and the customer-side
  fixtures would not notice.

### ✅ The correct fix — one line, and it does not touch the office

**Change `tierLine` at `:460-464` to use `tierNameOf` unconditionally:**

```ts
const tierLine = input.assessmentTier
  ? `You chose: ${tierNameOf(input.assessmentTier)}`
  : null;
```

**`assessmentSummary` stays, unchanged, for `:1214`.** ⚠️ **Then delete the
now-dead `settled !== null` ternary's justifying comment at `:450-458` or
rewrite it** — it explains a branch that no longer exists, and a comment that
survives its code is how the next reader re-derives the deleted behaviour.

⚠️ **State in the ticket, as a decision:** the customer's request email now names
the tier with **no figure**; the **office's** internal message keeps the figure.
That asymmetry is deliberate and must be pinned in both directions (see §Gate).

## §3 · 🔴 The resend defect — this ticket's adversarial target

**Verified exactly:** `resend.ts:94` — `if (appointment.status !== 'confirmed'
|| !hasEmail)` — refuses anything else, then `:106` calls `planForAppointment`,
which builds the **confirmed** message from **today's** constants.

So after the flip, the office clicks Resend on `#36` — `confirmed`, `cs_live_`,
**$628.43** — and that customer receives *"the visit is free… a travel fee may
apply… quoted separately"* **beside their real charge, with no refund policy at
all.** **Strictly worse than the pre-ticket defect**, which at least shipped a
refund policy of the wrong era. *(This is why decision 19's claim to have closed
R2-B2 was withdrawn: `APPROVED_*` is never resent by this route.)*

### DECIDED (revision 4, R4-3) — option (b)

`resend.ts`'s confirmed-message build **suppresses the terms block for any row
whose `terms_acked_at` predates the deploy**, and renders in its place:
*"The terms you accepted are in your original confirmation email."*

**Honest, and strictly better than both alternatives** — sending today's
free-mode terms beside a real $628.43 charge is false; sending nothing at all
contradicts `booking-email.ts:501-511`.

**Option (a), the terms snapshot at insert, is 🆕 BK-54** — an insert-path
change, and under decision 16 the one structure that survives an arbitrary
number of pricing flips. **BK-54 is not a gate on this ticket.**

### Three things the implementer must not get wrong

1. ⚠️ **R1-D2 rides here.** `settledAmountsOf` (**`booking-admin-notify.ts:220`**,
   not `booking-email.ts`) returns **null** for the thirteen migration-008
   pre-prepay rows, so any gate must be **three-state** (`null` / `0` / `>0`).
   Written `settled.totalCents > 0` it does not typecheck; written
   `settled?.totalCents > 0` it routes the null rows into the **FREE** branch.
2. ⚠️ **The deploy cutoff must be a value, not "now".** A comparison against
   deploy time evaluated at send time reclassifies every row the moment the
   clock passes it. **Pre-register the literal timestamp in this ticket** and
   assert against it.
3. ⚠️ **`planForAppointment` builds BOTH the customer and the office message.**
   Suppressing the terms block must not blank the office copy, which is the
   record the office reads back.
