# BK-53 — GATE 1: take every price off the site, and stop promising a refund

**Status:** draft — opened 2026-09-02. Not planned, not started.

**Phase:** free-booking changeover · **Tier:** **Reviewed** + adversarial pass —
this is customer-facing copy on public write paths. **Blocks:** **the push of
BK-51.** This is GATE 1.

## Why this exists

BK-51 makes the default outcome of every approval a **free confirmed booking**.
The site and every confirmation email still price and refund an assessment
nobody is charged for. **Until this ships, BK-51 cannot go live.**

| Surface | What it still says |
| --- | --- |
| `booking-copy.ts:401-405` `FEE_TERMS_ITEMS` → `ContactSection.astro:349`, `BookingForm.svelte:1115`, **and every confirmation email** (`booking-email.ts:525`) | `$399 + GST` / `$699 + GST` / `$1,199 + GST` |
| `booking-copy.ts:462` `FEE_TERMS_PAYMENT[2]` → `book.astro:143`, `ContactSection.astro:351`, `BookingForm.svelte:1119` | *"we email you a secure payment link, stating the amount and when we need it paid"* |
| `booking-copy.ts:504-510` `FEE_TERMS_REFUND` | four refund promises, in the same email as `NO_CHARGE_LINE` — *"There is no charge for this visit"* |
| `booking-copy.ts:541` `FEE_TERMS_ACK_LABEL` | asks the customer to tick *"including the amount I will be asked to pay"* |
| `/llms.txt:54` | the credited-in-full sentence — a price claim in words, no dollar sign |
| `ContactSection.astro:287` | *"Credited in full when you go ahead"* |

⚠ **Re-derive from `feeTermsPaymentFor`, NOT from the constant.**
`booking-email.ts:1150-1151` slices `FEE_TERMS_PAYMENT.slice(0,2)` for a
*confirmed* message — so `[2]` reaches the **request** email and the pages, while
the **confirmed** email carries `[0]` and `[1]` beside `NO_CHARGE_LINE`. Fixing
`[2]` alone ships `[0]` on the message the customer keeps.

## ⚠️ A LIVE CROSS-MODE DEFECT THIS TICKET MUST FIX, NOT INHERIT

**Emails already sent are frozen. The Resend button is not.**

`resend.ts:106` calls `planForAppointment(appointment, …, now)` — it re-renders
**today's constants** against a **stored row**. The numeric half is safe:
`paymentClaim` and `paidAmounts` (`booking-email.ts:412-413`, `:441`) derive from
`settled`, so a paid row still says "paid in full" and a $0 row still says
`NO_CHARGE_LINE`.

**The prose half is not gated at all.** `booking-email.ts:522-533` reads
`FEE_TERMS_ITEMS`, `feeTermsPaymentFor()`, `FEE_TERMS_REFUND` and
`FEE_TERMS_CREDIT` as **unconditional constants**.

**So the moment this ticket ships:** the office clicks Resend on a paid-era
confirmed booking — production has real ones, `#36` was a live `cs_live_` charge
of $628.43 — and that customer receives **free-mode terms, including a refund
policy that contradicts what they were actually charged.** Reachable today:
`resend.ts` is gated only on `status === 'confirmed'` plus a real email address.

**The fix is not a flag.** Row-gate the terms block on `settled.totalCents > 0`,
exactly the way `paymentClaim` at `:441` already is — the correct pattern is
eight lines above the defect. **Red-first it with a paid-era row.**

*(`terms_acked_at` records THAT the box was ticked, never WHAT it said. Once the
text changes there is no reconstruction but git history plus `created_at`. Not
made worse by this ticket, and a second reason the terms must be row-derived.)*
