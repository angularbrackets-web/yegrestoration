## Why this exists

BK-51 makes the default outcome of every approval a **free confirmed booking**.
The site and every confirmation email still price and refund an assessment
nobody is charged for. **Until this ships, BK-51 cannot go live.**

## ⚠️ A LIVE CROSS-MODE DEFECT THIS TICKET MUST FIX, NOT INHERIT

**Emails already sent are frozen. The Resend button is not.**

`resend.ts:106` calls `planForAppointment(appointment, …, now)` — it re-renders
**today's constants** against a **stored row**. The numeric half is safe:
`paymentClaim` and `paidAmounts` (`booking-email.ts:412-413`, `:441`) derive from
`settled`, so a paid row still says "paid in full" and a $0 row still says
`NO_CHARGE_LINE`.

**The prose half is not gated at all.** `booking-email.ts:522-533` reads
`FEE_TERMS_ITEMS`, `feeTermsPaymentFor()`, `FEE_TERMS_REFUND` and
`FEE_TERMS_CREDIT` as **unconditional constants**.

**So the moment this ticket ships:** the office clicks Resend on a paid-era
confirmed booking — production has real ones, `#36` was a live `cs_live_` charge
of $628.43 — and that customer receives **free-mode terms, including a refund
policy that contradicts what they were actually charged.** Reachable today:
`resend.ts` is gated only on `status === 'confirmed'` plus a real email address.

**The fix is not a flag.** Row-gate the terms block on `settled.totalCents > 0`,
exactly the way `paymentClaim` at `:441` already is — the correct pattern is
eight lines above the defect. **Red-first it with a paid-era row.**

*(`terms_acked_at` records THAT the box was ticked, never WHAT it said. Once the
text changes there is no reconstruction but git history plus `created_at`. Not
made worse by this ticket, and a second reason the terms must be row-derived.)*

## DELETE TO A BOUNDARY, NOT TO NOTHING

**Established 2026-09-02 by two design passes, against decision 16 (the client is
experimenting and may flip again).** This ticket as first drafted deletes the
pricing machinery permanently. That is the wrong shape for a model on its third
revision.

**The finding that decides it:** the money engine is **already mode-free.**
`approve()` computes a total and routes on `totalCents === 0`; BK-51 reduced the
entire free-vs-paid difference on that path to **one expression** — what the
amount field defaults to. The DB stores *amounts*, never a mode. A mixed
population of free and paid rows is already the design. **The only real
difference between the two models is static prose.**

**So: do the deletion, but delete to a boundary.**

| Do | Instead of |
| --- | --- |
| Keep `booking-pricing.ts` **intact and unreferenced**, and keep `verify-booking-pricing.ts` running against it | Deleting the tier ladder, the GST helper and 42 pinned checks |
| Route the terms prose through **one function with exactly one implementation** | Eight loose `FEE_TERMS_*` constants read directly by five surfaces and four email renders |
| Use **one named constant** (`ASSESSMENT_PRICES_SHOWN = false`) at the suppression sites, as a grep anchor | Deleting the sites and leaving nothing to grep for |
| **Delete `FEE_TERMS_REFUND` outright** | Gating it — a refund policy a config flip can switch on is a policy nobody reviewed |

**Do NOT write the paid-mode prose you don't need.** Sentences are what rot and
what a reviewer must weigh. **Do NOT add a mode column to the DB** — rows are
self-describing today (`payment_method='none'` + `paid_amount_cents=0`
discriminates cleanly, forever), and a mode column would make the one currently
reversible-by-construction thing permanent.

**The honest counter-argument, recorded because it nearly won:** a flag does not
reduce the work, it relocates it and doubles the state space of every assertion —
in a suite that BK-51 proved cannot catch a re-introduced defect under a
different spelling. **The resolution: the reversible thing is the STRUCTURE, not
the sentences.** What this buys is the elimination of archaeology — *"where did
this render, what did it say, what pinned it"* — which is the cost this repo's
own trap log says it keeps paying.

## What a flip back CANNOT undo

Classified 2026-09-02. Most fears here are unfounded; two are real.

**Fine — verified, stop worrying about these:**
- **DB rows.** Nothing stores a mode. A flip-back reads free rows correctly
  forever.
- **Structured data.** There is **no price anywhere in it** — `seo.ts`'s
  `hasOfferCatalog` carries `itemOffered` only; a grep for `priceRange`,
  `priceCurrency`, `lowPrice`, `highPrice` across `src/` returns **zero**.
  Nothing to remove, nothing to restore, no rich-result loss.
- **Stripe sitting unused 6–12 weeks.** Keys do not expire at that horizon (the
  only documented inactivity rule is 180 days and is scoped to payouts). No
  Products/Prices exist to archive — `booking-payment.ts` builds `price_data`
  inline. `verify-stripe-webhook.ts` stays green at 91/91 throughout.
- **The Google index.** `/book/` is in the sitemap; copy changes re-crawl
  normally.

**Real, and the second one is the sharp one:**
- **`/llms.txt` distribution.** `robots.txt` allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
  Google-Extended and CCBot. Training corpora do not retract. *Mild in practice*
  — the paid version is already in those corpora, so a flip-back is the
  better-supported direction.
- **⚠️ NOTHING SNAPSHOTS WHICH TERMS EACH BOOKING ACCEPTED.** `terms_acked_at`
  is a bare timestamp. Combined with the Resend defect above, **a flip in either
  direction rewrites the evidence of what past customers agreed to.** This is the
  single most expensive thing to get wrong here, it cannot be fixed
  retroactively, and it is cheap now: store a `terms_version` (or the rendered
  block) at insert in `booking-commit.ts`, and make `resend.ts` render from the
  snapshot.

## The drain step — required at ANY flip, in either direction

Before deploying, `pending_review` rows submitted under the **old** copy must be
resolved under the **old** rules. Otherwise a customer who read *"free"* gets a
payment link, or a customer holding a live Checkout Session with a
`payment_due_at` pays for what the site now calls free. Production population is
currently tiny — but **the check must be MADE at deploy time, not assumed.**

## Do NOT touch

`verify-cutover.ts`'s `BOOKED_CLAIM_SHAPES` and `ONSITE_PAYMENT_SHAPE`, and
`verify-booking-email.ts:365`'s ban on *"paid at the visit"*. **All are still
TRUE under a free assessment** and the temptation to invert them is the
documented wrong edit.
